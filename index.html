Questions, answered

The crunchy details.

Is LEGUMIO really gluten-free?+

The commercial product is intended to be gluten-free, but this prototype does not represent a certified product. Ingredient sourcing, cross-contact controls and finished-product testing must be completed before the claim appears on a saleable package.

Does Hot Honey-Style contain honey?+

The proposed vegan formulation uses plant-derived sweetness and no honey. “Hot Honey-Style” describes the sweet-heat flavor profile. Final naming must pass regulatory review for clarity.

What makes the company AI-first?+

Technology helps analyze flavor feedback, test concepts and forecast demand. It does not replace food scientists, safety experts, sensory panels or human judgment.

Are the protein and fiber claims final?+

No. They are development targets. Final values require the commercial formula, supplier data, accredited laboratory analysis and label review.

Can I actually place an order here?+

Not yet. The cart and checkout are a working prototype only. No payment is collected, inventory is simulated and orders are not submitted.
